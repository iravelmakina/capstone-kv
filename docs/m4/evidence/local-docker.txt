Note on D3: in this run the coordinator's line is missing from the first query. D2 had just
restarted the coordinator, and Alloy's Docker discovery refreshes every 60 s by default, so a
restarted container's logs reached Loki up to a minute late (nothing lost: they were
backfilled from Docker; the same query a minute later returned both the leader's and the
coordinator's line):
  capstone-kv-r2b-1         {"msg":"write", ... "request_id":"drill-1795773798","lsn":123, ...}
  capstone-kv-coordinator-1 {"msg":"request", ... "request_id":"drill-1795773798","principal":"ops","op":"write","status":200,"shard":"shard-2", ...}
Fix: `refresh_interval = "5s"` in monitoring/alloy.alloy. Re-measured after the fix: a
request made right after `docker compose restart coordinator` was in Loki 13 s after the
restart (about 6 s of it the coordinator starting up).

What the drills show against the design (ADR-0026/0029; heartbeat timeout 15 s):
- follower stopped: ReplicaDown and NodeDown in Alertmanager after 17-18 s (15 s heartbeat
  timeout + one scrape and evaluation); writes kept answering 200 (20 of 20, quorum of 2);
  both resolved 4 s after the start.
- coordinator stopped: CoordinatorDown after 15 s (probe and scrape fail at once, then
  `for: 10s`); resolved ~10 s after the start; writes 200 right after the restart (the M3
  bug fixed in c1a6c44 does not come back).
- one request followed: coordinator (principal, shard, key hash) -> leader (request ID ->
  LSN 123) -> both followers (appended range covering LSN 123).
